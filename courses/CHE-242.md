# CHE-242: Heat Transfer

| | |
|---|---|
| **Credit Hours** | 3-1 |
| **Prerequisites** | None |

## Course Objectives

To develop understanding of the concepts and laws of heat transfer for the design of heat transfer equipment.

## Course Contents

1. Conduction in steady-state and unsteady-state cases for one dimension.
2. Heat transfer by convection (natural & forced convection).
3. Application of dimensional analysis to convection.
4. Heat transfer by radiation.
5. Radiation from black and real surfaces; radiation between black surfaces; radiation between grey surfaces; radiation from gases.
6. Concept of film and overall heat transfer coefficients.
7. Unsteady-state heat transfer.
8. Heat transfer equipment: types and selection criteria.
9. Heat exchangers and their design.
10. Heat transfer with phase change: condensation and boiling heat transfer, and designing of single-component condensers.
11. Evaporation: heat transfer in evaporators, single-effect evaporators, multiple-effect evaporators, calculation of multiple-effect systems.
12. Comparison of forward and backward feeds, vapor compression evaporators, the heat pump cycle, evaporator operation, equipment for evaporation.

## Course Outcomes

After taking this course, students should have an understanding of the concepts and laws of heat transfer for the design of heat transfer equipment.

## List of Practicals

1. Calculate the efficiency of a double-pipe heat exchanger for parallel flow at different flow rates.
2. Calculate the efficiency of a double-pipe heat exchanger for counter flow at different flow rates.
3. Determine the relationship between flow rate and overall heat transfer coefficient in a double-pipe heat exchanger for parallel flow.
4. Determine the relationship between flow rate and overall heat transfer coefficient in a double-pipe heat exchanger for counter flow.
5. Calculate the efficiency of a shell & tube heat exchanger for parallel flow at different flow rates.
6. Calculate the efficiency of a shell & tube heat exchanger for counter flow at different flow rates.
7. Determine the relationship between flow rate and overall heat transfer coefficient in a shell and tube heat exchanger for parallel flow.
8. Determine the relationship between flow rate and overall heat transfer coefficient in a shell and tube heat exchanger for counter flow.
9. Show that the intensity of radiation on a surface is inversely proportional to the square of the distance of the surface from the radiation source (inverse square law).
10. Determine the emissivity of radiation surfaces with different finishes — polished, grey, and black.
11. Show that the intensity of radiation varies as the fourth power of the source temperature (Stefan-Boltzmann law).
12. Determine film-wise heat flux & surface heat transfer coefficient at constant pressure.
13. Determine drop-wise heat flux & surface heat transfer coefficient at constant pressure.
14. Study the effect of air inside the chamber of a film- and drop-wise condensation unit.
15. Demonstrate the various stages in flow boiling within the tube of a Flow Boiling Unit, including:
    - Convective heat transfer to sub-cooled liquid
    - Nucleation in sub-cooled and saturated liquid
    - Slugging
    - Annular flow
    - Droplet entrainment
    - Complete dry-out to superheated vapor
16. Demonstrate the circulation prompted by natural convection.
