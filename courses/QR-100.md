## Course Description

Quantitative Reasoning (I) is an introductory-level undergraduate course that focuses on the fundamentals related to quantitative concepts and analysis. The course is designed to familiarize students with the basic concepts of mathematics and statistics and to develop students' abilities to analyze and interpret quantitative information. Through a combination of theoretical concepts and practical exercises, this course will also enable students to cultivate their quantitative literacy and problem-solving skills while effectively expanding their academic horizon and breadth of knowledge in their specific major/field of study.
