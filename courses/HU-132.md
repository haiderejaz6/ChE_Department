## Course Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. Directly comprehend hundreds of Quranic sentences & verses.
2. Understand at least 80–85% of each page of the Holy Quran.
3. Understand common verses across different Quranic topics.
4. Achieve proficiency in the basic and advanced linguistic aspects of the Arabic language.
5. Understand the difference between Quranic verbs in various forms — present, past, and imperative.
6. Develop the ability to understand long verses of the Holy Quran independently and then comprehend their interpretation.

## Provision of Material, Content, and Books

- **Paper book:** All volumes available in printed form.
- **Tutorial videos:** Teaching video of each lesson available on YouTube.
- **Confirmation videos:** A complete series covering all lessons, allowing students to confirm their answers.
- **Flipbook:** A flipbook edition is also accessible.
- **Helping material:** Quizzes, question papers, and images available for teachers on the website.
