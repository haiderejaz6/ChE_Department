## Course Description

This course is designed to equip students with essential language skills for effective communication in diverse real-world scenarios. It focuses on developing proficiency in English language usage — word choices, grammar, and sentence structure. The course also enables students to grasp nuanced messages and tailor their communication effectively through comprehension and analytical skills in listening and reading. It encompasses a range of practical communication aspects including professional writing, public speaking, and everyday conversation, preparing students for both academic and professional spheres. A key focus is fostering an understanding of the impact of language on diverse audiences, enabling inclusive communication, cultural awareness, and effective functioning in a globalized world.

## Course Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. Develop proficiency in English language usage — appropriate word choices, grammar, and sentence structure — to improve written and spoken communication.
2. Comprehend nuanced messages and apply analytical skills in listening and reading to interpret and respond to diverse real-world scenarios.
3. Produce clear, concise, and professional written documents and deliver effective public speeches tailored to various audiences and purposes.
4. Engage in everyday conversations with ease and confidence.
5. Understand the impact of language on diverse audiences, communicating inclusively with cultural awareness.
6. Interact effectively in a globalized world across different cultural and professional contexts.
