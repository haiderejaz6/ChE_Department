## Course Description

The course comprises topics in physics directly related to engineering and technology. These include motion, friction, moment of inertia, oscillations, waves and propagation, electric charge & Coulomb's law, electric field, electric potential, capacitors & dielectrics, and magnetic fields.

The course aims to give students both a theoretical and a practical foundation for engineering courses such as Engineering Mechanics, Electromagnetic Field Theory, Systems and Signals, Control Systems, Transmission Lines, and Antennas & Microwave Devices. It gives students a sound knowledge of physics with applications to problems of a practical nature, enabling them to apply physics as a strong tool to understand and solve problems they encounter in engineering/technology.

## Teaching Plan

| Week | Topics | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Newton's Laws; Work, Energy | Applications of Newton's 1st, 2nd, and 3rd laws; work and energy. |
| 2–6 | Friction, Rotation, Oscillations, Moment of Inertia, Waves, Electric Charge, Electric Dipole, Electric Flux, Maxwell's 1st Equation (Gauss's Law) | Tension, normal, and frictional forces; dynamics of friction; uniform circular motion; kinematics of rotational motion; related problem solving. Simple harmonic motion, SHM and energy, damped and forced oscillations. Moment of inertia and wave propagation; energy and power carried by waves; reflection, interference, and diffraction. Charge and Coulomb's law; electric field and superposition principle; electric dipoles; electric flux; Gauss' law and different symmetries. |
| 7–8 | Electric Potential, Electric Current | Electric potential (point charges and dipole); electric potential from electric field and vice versa; conductors and equipotential surfaces; electric current, current density, drift velocity, resistance and resistivity, Ohm's law. |
| 9 | Mid Term Exam | |
| 10–12 | Resistance, Resistivity, Ohm's Law, Magnetic Force | Resistance and resistivity, Ohm's law (microscopic and macroscopic); magnetic force on a moving charge; magnetic force on current-carrying wires; torque on current loops and magnetic dipoles. |
| 13–17 | Magnetic Force on Current-Carrying Wires, Maxwell's 3rd and 4th Equations, Energy Stored in Capacitors and Inductors | Ampere's law and magnetic field due to long wires; magnetic field due to solenoid and toroid. Generators and motors; Faraday's law of induction; Lenz's law; motional EMF; induced electric field; capacitance; energy stored in an electric field; inductance; energy stored in a magnetic field. |
| 18 | End Semester Exams | |
