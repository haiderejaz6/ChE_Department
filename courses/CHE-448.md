# CHE-448: Process Intensification

| | |
|---|---|
| **Credit Hours** | 2 CH — Theory (2) |
| **Prerequisites** | Nil |
| **Category** | Elective |

## Course Contents (PEC Guidelines 2023–2024)

- Introduction to process intensification and its benefits.
- Techniques for process intensification in chemical engineering.
- **Process intensification in heat exchangers:** (a) Multi-stream heat exchanger, (b) compact heat exchanger.
- **Process intensification in reactors:** (a) Spinning disc reactor, (b) rotary packed-bed reactor, (c) oscillatory baffled reactor, (d) micro-reactor.
- **Process intensification in distillation columns:** (a) Divided-wall column, (b) Petlyuk column, (c) heat-integrated distillation column, (d) cyclic distillation.
