# MATH-351: Numerical Methods

| | |
|---|---|
| **Credit Hours** | 3-0 |
| **Prerequisites** | A-Level / F.Sc Physics |

## Course Description

The course gives students sound knowledge to solve non-linear equations numerically. Lengthy and subtle problems of differential calculus, integral calculus, and ordinary differential equations are also solved numerically. Curve fitting and interpolation topics are also included, which are very useful for engineers/technologists.

## Teaching Plan

| Week | Topics | Learning Outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mathematical Preliminaries | Round-off error and computer arithmetic; algorithms and convergence. |
| 2–6 | Iterative Methods for the Solution of Non-Linear Equations (convergence analysis); Interpolation | Bisection method, fixed-point method, Newton-Raphson method, secant method & Regula-Falsi method. Introduction, Lagrange interpolation, Newton's divided difference interpolation, forward and backward difference interpolation, introduction to cubic spline interpolation, clamped cubic spline, natural spline. |
| 7–8 | Numerical Differentiation and Integration | Numerical differentiation; elements of numerical integration — rectangular, trapezoidal, Simpson's 1/3 rule, Simpson's 3/8 rule, Weddle's rule. |
| 9 | Mid Term Exam | |
| 10–12 | Numerical Methods in Linear Algebra | LU factorization; Doolittle's, Crout's, and Cholesky's methods; iterative methods for systems of equations — Jacobi's method, Gauss-Seidel method; evaluation of eigenvalues by iteration (power method). |
| 13–17 | Solution of 1st and 2nd Order Ordinary Differential Equations; Solution of Higher Order IVPs, Elliptic PDEs, and Parabolic PDEs | Introduction, Euler method, Heun's method, Runge-Kutta methods. Solution of higher-order IVPs; solution of elliptic partial differential equations; solution of parabolic PDEs — Crank-Nicolson method. |
| 18 | End Semester Exams | |
