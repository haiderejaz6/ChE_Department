# CHE-451: Chemical Engineering Plant Design

| | |
|---|---|
| **Credit Hours** | 3 CH — Theory + Lab (3+0) |
| **Prerequisites** | Chemical Engineering Principles II, Mass Transfer |
| **Category** | Core Depth |

## Course Contents (PEC Guidelines 2023–2024)

- **Fundamentals:** General design considerations; design codes, standards, and materials selection.
- **Economic evaluation and optimization:** Basic concepts of cost indexing.
- **Process design:** Process design and development, process flow sheeting, flow diagrams, PFD and P&ID, equipment and instrument symbols.
- **Hazards identification and risk assessment:** Process hazards checklists and surveys, safety reviews.
- **Applications — stationary equipment:** Vessel design; low-, medium-, and high-pressure storage and transportation vessels; cryogenic vessels.
- **Rotating equipment** including pumps, motors, compressors, and turbines.
- **Design ethics:** Local and global impact analysis.
- **Applications — stationary equipment (continued):** Heat transfer equipment including furnaces and refrigeration units; piping and pipeline design.
- **Design of mass transfer equipment;** material transport; material handling.
