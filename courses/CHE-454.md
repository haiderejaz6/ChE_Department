# CHE-454: Process Modelling, Simulation and Optimization

| | |
|---|---|

| **Category** | Core Depth |

## Course Contents (PEC Guidelines 2023–2024)

- **Design & simulation software:** Introduction to various design and simulation software (e.g., HYSYS, ChemCAD, etc.); review of capabilities and limitations of the design/simulation software; flowsheets and sub-flowsheets.
- Defining process streams and use of fluid packages.
- Adding common unit operations to the flow sheet; drawing simple Process Flow Diagrams (PFD) in HYSYS; steady-state material and energy balances using the graphical user interface and worksheet.
- **Simulation of equipment** from the following categories in Aspen HYSYS: heat transfer, separators, pressure changers, reactors.
- Economic analysis in the simulation environment.
- Hierarchy of process design; process synthesis and design strategy; strategies for decision making.
- **Importance and hierarchy of optimization:** Significance of optimization in chemical engineering; classification and model development; solution and interpretation of optimization models; economic and time value of objective functions.
- **Linear programming application** to chemical processes in multi-variant situations: unconstrained functions with one-dimensional search, nonlinear mixed-integer optimization, application of optimization in heat transfer and energy conservation.
- **Optimal design and operation of conventional mass transfer operations:** Optimal design of fluid flow in pipes with and without pumping and compressing devices; optimization of medium-scale plants along with integrated planning and control in process industries.
