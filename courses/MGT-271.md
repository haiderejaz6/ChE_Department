| | |

## Course Description

Entrepreneurship is an examination of the fundamentals of starting and managing a small business, the role of the entrepreneur, and entrepreneurial characteristics. The course includes exposure to the opportunities and pitfalls of entrepreneurship as well as methods to take advantage of the former and avoid the latter.
