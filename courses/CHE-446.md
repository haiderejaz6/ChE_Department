| | |
|---|---|

## Course Contents (PEC Guidelines 2023–2024)

- **Advanced fuel technologies:** Sustainability, recycling, carbon-free fuels, next-generation fuels.
- **Methanol & ethanol:** Historical development and production of methanol; reactions and applications; future opportunities and challenges; storage and handling aspects; pure methanol and methanol-containing systems; toxicity, occupational health, and environmental concerns.
- **Production of methanol:** Thermodynamics and kinetics of methanol synthesis; syngas preparation; steam reforming of natural gas to methanol; conversion of methanol to gasoline and to olefins; methanol as a fuel; methanol vehicle exhaust emissions; future methanol engines and vehicles; methanol in heavy-duty engines; outlook for fuel methanol.
- **Hydrogen:** An energy-dependent world; the basics of hydrogen — history and development; why hydrogen as a fuel; pros and cons into the future. Production of hydrogen from fossil electrolysis, coal, methane, and biomass pyrolysis/steam reforming; modeling of hydrogen separation; storage and handling aspects; membranes for enhanced hydrogen production from the water-gas shift reaction; hydrogen-metal systems; mass storage of hydrogen.
- **Hydrogen storage for future energy systems:** Fuel cell system model; transportation of gaseous hydrogen by pipelines; transportation of liquid hydrogen by truck or ship; analysis and simulations; fuel cells; progress in PEM fuel cell development; hydrogen-fueled transportation; fuel cell vehicles.
- **Hydrazine:** Introduction and physical properties of hydrazine chemistry; production of hydrazine; handling aspects; decomposition and combustion of hydrazine; hydrazine applications.
