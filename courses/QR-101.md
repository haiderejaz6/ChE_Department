## Course Description

Quantitative Reasoning (II) is a sequential undergraduate course that focuses on logical reasoning supported by mathematical and statistical concepts and modeling/analysis techniques, equipping students with the analytical skills and critical thinking abilities necessary to navigate the complexities of the modern world. The course familiarizes students with the quantitative concepts and techniques required to interpret and analyze numerical data, and cultivates the logical reasoning needed to construct and evaluate arguments, identify fallacies, and think systematically. Building on the prerequisite course of Quantitative Reasoning (I), this course enables students to further their quantitative, logical, and critical reasoning abilities to complement their specific major/field of study.
