# ME-105: Workshop Practice

| | |
|---|---|
| **Credit Hours** | 0-1 |

## Course Description

Students are introduced to hands-on training at different workshop sections, including the machine shop, welding and fabrication shop, woodwork shop & pattern making, bench fitting shop, surface treatment shop, electrical shop, and foundry and forging shop. The main objectives of this course are:

1. To introduce basic manufacturing processes through workshop practices.
2. To apply the acquired knowledge in practice to manufacture products/components.
3. To inculcate teamwork through projects/tasks.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to Workshop Technology | Definitions and terminologies; process of manufacturing; industrial safety; industrial materials; manufacturing standards; quality control. | 1 |

| 3 | Bench Fitting Practice | Fits and tolerances; filing work, jigs and fixtures, taps and die work; drilling and grinding, marking and punching. | 3 |
| 4 | Machining Practice (Lathe) | Types of lathe machines and operations; cutting tools, accessories, and attachments; parts of lathe machines; safety precautions. | 3 |
| 5 | Machining Practice (Milling) | Types of milling machines and operations; cutting tools, accessories, and attachments; parts of milling machines; safety precautions. | 3 |
| 6 | Pattern Making / Wood Work | Introduction to wood and classification; seasoning of wood; engineering applications of wood; properties of wood and wood joints; pattern making, wood defects; woodworking tools and machines. | 3 |
| 7 | Forging Work | Forging tools; hot and cold forging; properties and crystal structure of metals; forging types/operations; safety precautions. | 2 |
| 8 | Foundry Work | Introduction to foundry; different methods of casting including latest techniques; different types of furnaces; mold and die casting; casting defects; safety precautions. | 3 |
| 9 | Electrical Technology | Basic electrical technology; power supply circuits, types of cables and insulators; electrical tools and instruments; basic fault diagnosis in circuits; electrical devices; electrical shock prevention and treatment; electrical safety precautions. | 3 |
| 10 | Welding Technology | Introduction to welding theory; types of welding, welding joints; ARC welding techniques; gas welding techniques; safety precautions. | 6 |
| 11 | Surface Treatment | Electroplating processes; electroplating techniques; preparation of workpiece (degreasing and pickling, etc.); solution preparation for plating and related environmental issues. | 2 |
| 12 | Paint Work | Paints and application; primers and solvents; types of paint; color combination. | 1 |
| 13 | Heat Treatment | Introduction to heat treatment; heat treatment cycle; heat treatment furnaces and their types; heat treatment process. | 1 |
| 14 | Term Project/Case Study/OEP/Practical Test | | 6 |
