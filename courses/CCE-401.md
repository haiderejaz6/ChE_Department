## Course Description

This course is designed to provide students with fundamental knowledge about civics, citizenship, and community engagement. Students will learn about the essentials of civil society, government, civic responsibilities, and inclusivity, along with effective ways to participate in shaping society — helping them apply theoretical knowledge to real-world situations to make a positive impact on their communities.
