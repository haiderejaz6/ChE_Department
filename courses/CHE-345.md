# CHE-345: Transport Phenomena

| | |
|---|---|
| **Credit Hours** | 3-0 |
| **Prerequisites** | CHE-221: Fluid Mechanics-I |

## Course Objectives

- To relate and express the basics of transport phenomena in terms of vectors and tensors.
- To set up and solve microscopic momentum, energy, and mass balances for given systems.
- To demonstrate and apply the equations of change for given systems.
- To couple flow hydrodynamics, reaction kinetics, and heat transfer to solve complex engineering problems.
- To develop the ability to define engineering problems, explore solutions, and critically analyze them to achieve a practical solution.

## Course Contents

1. Introduction to vectors and tensors and their application in fluid flow.
2. Transfer processes: a review of the mechanisms of momentum, energy, and mass transport. Balance principles for momentum, energy, and mass.
3. Momentum transport: diffusivity and mechanism of momentum transport. Derivation of equations of continuity and motion (Navier-Stokes) for 1D, 2D, and 3D.
4. Application of Navier-Stokes equations to solve problems related to fluid flow for the 1D and 2D cases.
5. Energy transport: thermal conductivity and mechanism of energy transport. Derivation of the energy equation at the microscopic level. Application to heat transfer problems involving conduction, free/forced convection, and radiation. Interphase transport in non-isothermal systems.
6. Case study: temperature distribution profiles for 1D and 2D cases.
7. Mass transport: diffusivity and mechanism of mass transport. Derivation of species conservation equations for binary and multi-component mixtures.
8. Case studies applying mass transport equations to model species concentration profiles and changes along the length of a reactor (convection-diffusion equation).
9. Introduction to transport in turbulent flow: fluctuations and time-averaged quantities. Time-averaged form of the governing equations of momentum, energy, and mass transport.

## Course Outcomes

After completing this course, students will be able to:

- Demonstrate a fundamental understanding of the basic equations that describe momentum, energy, and mass transport.
- Formulate macroscopic mass, momentum, and energy balances, and apply dimensional analysis to solve engineering problems related to fluid flow.
- Solve the equation of motion, equation of continuity, and Navier-Stokes equations to analyze engineering problems related to Newtonian fluid flow in laminar flow.
- Develop and understand the equations of change which describe how mass, energy, momentum, and angular momentum change within a small region.
- Determine the velocity, temperature, and concentration profiles of different systems.
- Apply numerical and analytical problem-solving skills and techniques in the field of mass, energy, momentum, and angular momentum.
- Use the equations of change to solve transport phenomena problems.
- Calculate heat transfer coefficients for different systems.
- Determine the heat transfer rate for single and composite walls.
- Estimate molar/mass flux and concentration profiles for steady-state and unsteady-state molecular diffusion.
